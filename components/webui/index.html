Install an app

    
An app package: a .zip, or a folder, holding app.json, main.lua and
      optionally icon.png (turned into the 32×32 black-and-white icon here). Installing an app that's
      already there updates it and keeps its settings and saved data. How to write one: docs/api.md.

Add a book

    
A .txt (UTF-8 or GB18030/GBK), .epub or .html file. It's converted here in
      the browser to plain text the device can show (no images, tidy paragraphs, only characters its font has),
      with chapters marked from the book's table of contents or its chapter headings, then sent in small pieces.
