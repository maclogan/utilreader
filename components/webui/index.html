Apps

Install an app

    
An app package: a .zip, or a folder, holding app.json, main.lua and
      optionally icon.png (turned into the 32×32 black-and-white icon here). Installing an app that's
      already there updates it and keeps its settings and saved data. How to write one: docs/api.md.

    

      
      
    

    

      

      
    

  


  

    
App catalog

    
Apps shared by others. The list comes straight from the catalog's web address (your
      browser fetches it); installing checks each download against the catalog's checksum.

Add a book

    
A .txt (UTF-8 or GB18030/GBK), .epub or .html file. It's converted here in
      the browser to plain text the device can show (tidy paragraphs, only characters its font has),
      with chapters marked from the book's table of contents or its chapter headings, then sent in small pieces.
      An e-book's cover and pictures come along as small greyscale images, each on a page of its own.

    
    
Pick several to add them all at once, or drop files anywhere on this page: books, music,
      app packages (.zip), podcast lists (.opml) and pictures each go where they belong.

    

      

      
      

      

        
        

        
      

    

  


  

    
Off screen picture

    
A photo to show while the device is off, in 4 shades of grey. Choose the part to show here
      in the browser (the screen is 296 × 128). Takes effect the next time the device turns off.

    
    

      
Drag the frame to choose what shows; the slider (or the mouse wheel) sizes it.

SD card
